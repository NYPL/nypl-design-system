## Overview

`Grid` is directly exported from Chakra UI. It's `Box` with `display: grid` and
it comes with helpful style shorthand. It offers more complexity and
formatting options than the Reservoir Design System (DS) [`SimpleGrid`](/?path=/docs/components-page-layout-simplegrid--docs)
component.

Details about available props and related child components can be found on Chakra's
[Grid component](https://v2.chakra-ui.com/docs/components/grid/usage) page.

{

<Grid templateColumns="repeat(5, 1fr)" gap="grid.default" mb="s">
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.primary" />
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.secondary" />
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.primary" />
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.secondary" />

  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.secondary" />

  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.secondary" />
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.primary" />
  <Box w="100%" h="20" bg="brand.secondary" />
</Grid>
}

## Complex layouts with `Grid` children

You may need certain grid items to span a specific amount of columns or rows
instead of an even distribution. To achieve this, you can pass the `colSpan` and
`rowSpan` props. You'll also need to specify the `templateColumns` and `templateRows`.

{

<Grid
  height="200px"
  templateRows="repeat(2, 1fr)"
  templateColumns="repeat(5, 1fr)"
  gap="grid.default"
>
  <GridItem rowSpan={2} colSpan={1} bg="ui.link.primary" />
  <GridItem colSpan={2} bg="ui.success.primary" />
  <GridItem colSpan={2} bg="ui.success.secondary" />
  <GridItem colSpan={4} bg="ui.link.secondary" />
</Grid>
}
